# Brightpath Academy – Student Handbook (fictional demo data)

## Courses and fees
- Python Foundations: 3 months, fee Rs. 12,000.
- Data Analytics: 5 months, fee Rs. 25,000.
- Full-Stack Web Development: 6 months, fee Rs. 30,000.

## Batches
Weekday batch: Monday to Friday, 6 PM to 8 PM. Weekend batch: Saturday and Sunday, 10 AM to 1 PM. New batches start on the first Monday of every month.

## Eligibility
Python Foundations has no prerequisites. Data Analytics and Full-Stack Web Development require basic computer knowledge. Students must be 16 years or older.

## Scholarships
A 10% scholarship is available to students who score above 85% in the entrance assessment. Only one scholarship can be applied per student.

## Refund policy
- Cancellation within 7 days of the batch start date: 90% of the fee is refunded.
- Cancellation between day 8 and day 14: 50% of the fee is refunded.
- No refund after day 14.
- Refunds are processed within 10 working days.

## Contact
Email: support@brightpath.example. Office hours: 10 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.

## How to enroll
Students can enroll by telling the assistant which course they want. After the student confirms their name and mobile number, an academy counsellor calls them to complete admission.
